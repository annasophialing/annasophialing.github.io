about

i'm a sophomore at rice university, majoring in mechanical engineering with a computational engineering specialization, and minoring in computational and applied mathematics and spanish.

i came to college as a pre-med neuroscience major and switched to engineering during orientation week, with no technical experience. freshman year i was learning everything from scratch, and it just so happened that i loved it!

i'm most drawn to work where math meets real systems, and i like understanding how things work and then building them myself. outside of class i'm usually lifting (six pull-ups and counting), doing hot yoga, shooting videos, adding to my 200+ spotify playlists, or ranking restaurants on beli.
